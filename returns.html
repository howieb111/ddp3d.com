Returns & Shipping Issues

We want you to be happy with your order. Here's how we handle damage, mistakes, defects, and plain old returns.

If your order arrives damaged

Shipping damage happens sometimes, even with careful packaging. Email support@ddp3d.com within 3 days of delivery with a short video showing the damaged item and the shipping box together, with your order number and the date written on a note visible in frame. Carriers require this kind of evidence to process a claim, and it's the fastest way for us to sort things out.

We'll review the video and let you know the next step — for confirmed shipping damage, that's a prepaid return label, at no cost to you. We decide the remedy (repair, replacement, or refund) once we've received and inspected the returned item; we don't issue a replacement or refund on video alone. Minor cosmetic damage that doesn't affect how the product works is handled differently than functional damage — usually a partial refund rather than a full replacement.

If we shipped the wrong item

Standard, ready-made products: email support@ddp3d.com and we'll ship the correct item right away at no cost to you. No need to return the incorrect one — keep it or donate it.

Custom or bulk orders: we'll send a prepaid return label for the incorrect item. Once we've received and inspected the returned item, we'll ship the correct order at no cost to you.

Manufacturing defects

If something you ordered fails or arrives with a manufacturing defect that isn't related to shipping damage, email support@ddp3d.com; the same video-first process above applies. We'll repair, replace, or refund once we've reviewed the issue.

Quality & manufacturing notes

We strive for the highest quality, and aim to complete each project using raw material from the same manufacturing batch so color stays consistent throughout. Occasionally there will be very fine variations within a print, or between prints in the same batch. For functional prints, we generally consider this kind of variation acceptable; for decorative prints, we don't — those would be treated as rejects.

If your order requires zero variation within a print or across an order, email sales@ddp3d.com to discuss before ordering. Depending on the order, matching that requirement may take extra production time and delay other orders, so we may need to charge a fee to cover that.

Change of mind or ordered the wrong option

If you'd like to return something you ordered — wrong choice, changed your mind, no hard feelings — you have 30 days from delivery to send it back. The item must be unused and in new condition, in its original packaging where possible. Return shipping is at your cost, and original shipping charges, if any, are non-refundable. Once we receive and inspect the return, we'll refund the product price to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Custom and bulk orders are made to your specification and are final sale — this section doesn't apply to them.

Custom & bulk orders

Custom or bulk orders are made to order and are final sale for buyer's remorse or ordering the wrong spec. Damage, our mistakes, and manufacturing defects are still covered under the sections above.

Questions

Email support@ddp3d.com with your order details and we'll get it sorted.
