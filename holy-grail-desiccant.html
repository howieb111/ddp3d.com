Holy Grail Desiccant Containers

Bottom-loading desiccant containers built as a companion to Holy Grail-style filament dry boxes. Each one holds up to 100g of desiccant — a big load area that means less-frequent recharging — with a secure clip-on bottom cover and an optional funnel that eliminates spillover when refilling.

Get it on MakerWorld →

What's included

A ready-to-print .3mf for the Bambu H-Series is published on MakerWorld, and separate STL files for every component are included so you can assemble containers for any printer or dry box. Use one container, front and back, or a matched pair — each one keeps its own filament spool separated and holds up to 100g of desiccant behind a secure, clip-on cover.

The story

Built as a tribute to the Holy Grail Filament Dry Boxes, this design started from a simple frustration: most desiccant containers are small, top-loading, and easy to spill while refilling. These load from the bottom instead, hold more desiccant per fill, and pair with an optional funnel so refilling stays clean. A secure clip-on cover keeps everything in place — assembly takes a little practice the first time, but once you've done it once it's quick.

Free and open source — no account, no cost, just download and print. Questions or ideas for improvements are welcome at info@ddp3d.com.
