Free & open source

Glassy McBuildPlate

A fully parameterized top glass and build plate holder that clips onto the side of your printer, keeping spare build plates and tempered glass organized and within reach. Every dimension is adjustable, so it fits any printer's build plates — not just one model.

Parameterize & print on MakerWorld → Download Fusion source →

What's included

Two ways to get it, depending on how hands-on you want to be. The MakerWorld page lets you adjust every parameter — wall width, plate count, section heights, backstop offset, and more — right in the browser and export a ready-to-slice model, no CAD software required. The Fusion 360 source file gives you the fully editable parametric model if you want deeper control or want to fold it into your own design.

The story

Every printer ships with one build plate, but most people end up with a small stack of spares — textured, smooth, engineering — plus a sheet of tempered glass for the surfaces that need it. Glassy McBuildPlate started as a way to keep that stack organized and protected next to the printer instead of leaning against a wall, and it's fully parameterized so it can be resized to fit whatever build plates you're actually storing.

This one's released free and open source — no account, no cost, just download and print. Questions or ideas for improvements are welcome at info@ddp3d.com.
