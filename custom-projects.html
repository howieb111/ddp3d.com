Custom Design, Development & Printing

Every product ddp3d sells today started the same way — as a custom project solving one specific problem. The fly-fishing stripping basket and the hymnal rack pencil & card holder were both designed, prototyped, and refined for a single customer before they became standard products. That same process is available for your project too, whether it needs one part or a production run.

Start a project → See what we've built

How it works

Custom work is handled as an engagement, not an instant order — every project starts with a conversation.

  1. Tell us the problem

    Submit the form below with what you're trying to build or solve — the more detail (photos, sketches, dimensions, constraints), the better. This doesn't generate an automatic quote.

  2. We design & prototype

    We engineer a solution in CAD and print a working prototype, invoiced directly through Stripe. No payment details are ever collected on this site.

  3. We iterate

    You test the prototype in the real world and tell us what to adjust. Most projects take at least one more round to get right.

  4. We produce

    Once the design holds up, we print your order — a single part or a full production run — and invoice at a rate that matches the quantity.

What we can help with

Brackets, mounts, and enclosures. Jigs and fixtures for a shop or studio. Replacement parts for something discontinued or broken. Accessories that extend a product you already own or sell. Small-batch production runs for a business, team, or organization. If it's a problem that a well-engineered, 3D-printed part could solve, it's worth asking about.

Parts are designed in Fusion 360 and printed in-house in a range of materials — PLA, PETG, and marine-grade ASA among them — in whatever color and finish fits the job. Every design is tested before it ships, the same way the products in our catalog were.
