Custom Pencil & Card Holders

A custom pencil and card holder built around what you need — your logo, a non-standard card or brochure size, or a mounting point our fixed Standard line doesn't cover. It started as a discreet, no-drilling mount for a pew hymnal rack — already in use at a local congregation — but the same design-and-prototype process works for any custom fit.

Smooth or textured. Matte or reflective. Many colors available to match your environment.

Get in touch → Pricing below.

Just need a fixed size, fast? See the instant-checkout Standard Pencil & Card Holder — or the Card / Brochure Holder. No design review needed.

Where this started — one example

During the service at one congregation, members fill out a card marking a Joy or a Sorrow they'd like to share. The cards and pencils used to just sit loose on the pew cushions — the pencils would roll off, and the cards would slide down and get lost, leaving the ushers unhappy about cleaning them up. These holders clean things up and are easily accessible to everyone in the pew.

These aren't limited to Joy and Sorrow cards — they can hold anything you'd want a parishioner to fill out before, during, or after the service. They can also be customized to hold only information cards or brochures instead, with your logo built into the mold, sized and mounted however your space requires. This is just one example of the custom process — anything is possible.

How ordering works

Every custom job is a little different, so orders are handled as a short engagement rather than an instant checkout.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Submit the form below with your estimated quantity and any custom details — a logo to include, a non-standard size, or a mounting point that doesn't match our fixed Standard line. We'll follow up by email or phone to talk through sizing, color, and finish — this doesn't generate an automatic quote.

  2. We invoice each prototype round

    We send you a prototype so you can check the fit and logo placement in person, and invoice for each prototype round by email through Stripe. Most engagements need at least two rounds — sometimes the second prototype is spot on and can reduce the size of your final order.

  3. We invoice your order

    Once the prototype checks out, we send a final Stripe invoice at the matching bulk rate below. No payment details are ever collected on this site — invoices are paid directly and securely through Stripe.
