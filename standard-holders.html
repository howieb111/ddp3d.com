Standard Card, Brochure & Pencil Holders

Fixed sizes, ready to ship — no design review, no waiting on a quote. Pick hanging or tabletop, measure your rail if you're hanging it, and check out instantly. We recommend ordering one first to confirm the fit before ordering the quantity you need.

How to measure

If you're mounting to a rail or ledge (Hanging), the only measurement that matters is its thickness. You want a loose, comfortable grip — snug enough to stay in place, but not so tight that it stresses the plastic. Too thin and it won't hold; too thick and it won't fit at all. Tabletop needs no measuring at all.
