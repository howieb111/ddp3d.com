About ddp3d

ddp3d is a small design shop making practical, 3D-printed accessories for fishing, biking, music, worship, and everyday life around the house.

Every project starts as a problem worth solving: a stripping basket that outlasts saltwater and sun, a bike mount that holds up mile after mile, a hymnal rack that finally has somewhere to put a pencil. Designs get modeled, printed, tested, and iterated until they hold up in the field — not just on screen.

Some designs are sold direct, order by order. Others are released free and open source, for anyone with a printer to download and make their own.

Design

Every product starts as a real problem, modeled and dimensioned with intent.

Develop

Prototypes get printed, tested, and refined before anything ships.

Print

Produced on demand — sold direct, or released for anyone to print themselves.
