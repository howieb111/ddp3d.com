The story

Most stripping baskets are injection-molded, one-size-fits-all, and start to fade and crack after a season or two of sun and saltwater. This one is different: printed in marine-grade ASA in three wall heights, so you can match the basket to how and where you actually fish.

Made locally in the USA, it holds its color and resists UV and saltwater breakdown far longer than injection-molded baskets. The one part that ever needs replacing — the zip-tie line separators — swaps in the field in seconds.

Built for the water

Made in the USA

Designed, printed, and finished in-house — not mass-produced overseas.

Marine-grade ASA

Holds its color and resists UV fading, chalking, and saltwater breakdown far longer than injection-molded plastic.

Field-replaceable line separators

The zip-tie "fingers" swap out in seconds if one ever breaks — no tools, no waiting on a warranty claim.

Polyester waist belt

Won't soak up water, stay cold and wet against you, or fray and rot with repeated saltwater exposure the way nylon does.

Edge zip ties prevent spillover

Mounted flat-side-forward around the rim so line stays in the basket and nothing catches your line on the way out.

Belt fits 32"–48" waist

Adjustable out of the box; smaller waists can trim and refinish the belt to fit.

Fast-drain design

Chamfered drain holes empty the basket in about 1.35 seconds — shaped so they won't nick or fray your line.

Warranty

Every basket shell carries a Limited Lifetime Warranty covering manufacturing defects and material failure under normal use. It doesn't cover damage from abuse — impacts, crushing, or stacked weight. The zip-tie line separators are a user-replaceable wear item rather than a warranty item, which is why spares ship with every basket.
