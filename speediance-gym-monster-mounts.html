What's included

  • Bolt-on clamp: A knurled knob and bolt (included) lock each mount to the rail. If it ever loosens, tighten the bolt — the threads have extra length for that. Bend relief in the clamp arms makes install and removal easier.
  • Barbell and rowing bar holder: Two cradles on each mount, angled away from you, for safety, hold the barbell and the rowing bar.
  • Barbell foam pad holder (Medium and Large): Two short arms hold the pad.
  • Up to 3 hook sets per mount: Small has 1 set per mount (6 hooks total) and Medium has 2 sets (12 hooks total), each with a front-facing, rear-facing, and side-facing hook. Large keeps those same 2 full hook sets and adds a third set with a front-facing hook plus two flat, multi-purpose mounts in place of the rear and side hooks — better suited to straps, though a carabiner still fits fine (14 hooks + 4 multi-purpose mounts total across the pair). Between the hooks and multi-purpose mounts, your handles, ropes, ankle straps, D-rings and extender belts each get their own spot.
  • Side hooks: Keep wide accessories, like tricep ropes, hanging narrow when you use a carabiner, just like they would without one. A carabiner turns whatever it holds a quarter turn, which makes wide accessories hang wide — the side-facing hook turns it back.
  • D-ring friendly: Plenty of finger clearance around the bolt and nut, so accessories can hang directly by their D-rings — or use a carabiner as a quick-swap go-between if you prefer. See the FAQ below for details.
  • Durable PETG: Printed in PETG, a tougher and more flexible plastic than standard PLA — it resists cracking and flexes slightly under load instead of snapping, which suits a clamp that gets tightened and hooks that carry real weight.
  • Colors: Black, Gray, Red, Blue, Orange and Green.

Use & care

  • Storage only: Not an anchor point. Never attach a cable, band, or strap to the mount to pull against, and don't hang from it or use it for pull-ups.
  • Load limits: Hooks are rated for 5 lb each hanging long-term. The barbell and rowing bar cradles are made for the Speediance bars that come with the Gym Monster.
  • Check the clamp: Every so often, make sure it's snug and hand-tighten the bolt if needed. If a part ever cracks or deforms, stop using it and contact me.
  • Heat: Keep it out of extreme heat — a hot car, or a garage in direct summer sun. Normal indoor use is fine. If you need it to handle extreme heat, contact me and I can offer an upgrade to ABS.
  • Kids: Keep children from climbing on or hanging from the machine or the mounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hang accessories directly by their D-rings, or do I need a carabiner?

Either works. Speediance accessories come with a D-ring already attached for connecting to the cable, and the mounts leave plenty of finger clearance around the bolt and nut, so you can hook D-rings on and off directly. If you'd rather use carabiners, they fit fine too — and the side-facing hook keeps wide accessories like tricep ropes hanging narrow even on a carabiner.

Why does the Gym Monster clip use a bolt instead of tape?

All 3D printed clips relax their grip a bit over time under stress — it's just how plastic behaves. Many competitors use double-sided tape to compensate. It's not that I don't trust tape — it's just a pain to work with: it doesn't always look great, it's hard to reposition once placed, and it leaves residue behind. A bolt avoids all of that. If it ever feels loose, just hand-tighten it a bit, no tools needed. A hook may also sag very slightly over time depending on load, especially in a hot garage in summer.

How much weight can each hook hold?

Each hook is rated for 5 lb hanging long-term — plenty for handles, ropes, straps, and a weight belt. They'll take much more briefly without breaking, but constant heavy loads can make a hook sag slightly over time, especially somewhere hot, so keep anything left hanging at 5 lb or less per hook.

What can the barbell and rowing bar cradles hold?

They're made for the Speediance barbell and rowing bar that come with the Gym Monster — one resting across each pair of cradles. Please don't rack heavier bars from other equipment, or lean on or hang from a bar while it's resting in the cradles.

Have a different home gym?

These mounts are designed around the Gym Monster, but I can design something like them for other machines — or work out a different rail profile, a different accessory to mount, or a color combination outside the six offered here. See Custom Design, Development & Printing and let's talk.
